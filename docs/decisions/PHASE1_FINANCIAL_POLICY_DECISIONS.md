## Gate outcome

Phase 1 can implement policy-independent serialization and preserve the already-defined one-active-digital-session rule. Mixed partial methods are retained because current canonical behavior already permits partial payments and no incompatible exclusivity rule exists; this document does not approve new methods or refund initiation. Full refund/void/reversal behavior remains stopped pending product approval.

An ambiguous outcome may be resolved only by authoritative success, authoritative definitive failure/cancellation/expiry, or an operator-approved future reconciliation procedure. The operator procedure itself remains outside this phase; Phase 1 must preserve enough immutable evidence and must not release the money meanwhile.
